# 🧮 Refined Premium Model — Zoink-4-u Actuarial Plan v2.0

## Overview

The Zoink-4-u premium model moves beyond simple flat-rate pricing to a **Dynamic Gig-Risk Index (DGRI)**. This ensures that a delivery partner's premium is a precise reflection of their individual exposure, platform pressure, and environmental risk, while remaining strictly within their "salary count" (affordability).

---

## 1. The Master Equation

The weekly premium ($P_w$) is calculated using a non-linear exposure-weighted formula:

$$P_w = \text{Base} \times \left( \sum W_i R_i \right) \times \Phi(WH, DT) \times \Omega(TS)$$

| Component | Meaning | Function |
|-----------|---------|----------|
| **Base** | Tier Base Rate | Standardized entry cost (₹29, ₹45, ₹69) |
| **$\sum W_i R_i$** | Weighted Risk Pool | Combines Zone, Season, Platform, and Scenario risks |
| **$\Phi(WH, DT)$** | Exposure Multiplier | Non-linear risk based on hours and time-of-day |
| **$\Omega(TS)$** | Trust/Behavioral Factor | Discount or penalty based on GigShield Trust Score |

---

## 2. Parameter Definitions (The Inputs)

### A. Base Rate (Tier-Based)
*   **🥉 Bronze (₹29):** High-deductible, essential coverage for part-timers.
*   **🥈 Silver (₹45):** Balanced coverage for regular full-timers.
*   **🥇 Gold (₹69):** Low-deductible, maximum payout for "super-riders" in high-risk zones.

### B. Weighted Risk Pool ($\sum W_i R_i$)
We use four primary risk vectors:
1.  **Zone Risk ($R_z$):** Historical disruption frequency of the pincode (Normalized 0.8 to 1.8).
2.  **Seasonal Factor ($R_s$):** Periodic weather cycles (e.g., Monsoon = 1.4, Dry = 0.9).
3.  **Platform Volatility ($R_p$):** 
    - **Zomato/Swiggy:** 1.0x (Standard food delivery pressure).
    - **Zepto/Blinkit/BigBasket:** 1.15x (Higher risk due to 10-min delivery SLAs and stricter penalties for rain-delays).
4.  **Scenario Risk Score ($R_{sc}$):** A dynamic 7-day look-ahead score generated by our **AI Disruption Forecaster**.
    - If a cyclone or strike is predicted: $R_{sc}$ increases by 0.2 to 0.5 for that specific week.

### C. Exposure Multiplier ($\Phi$) — Unique Logic
Risk is not linear with hours. A worker operating during a thunderstorm at night is at higher risk than one working a sunny afternoon.
*   **WH (Work Hours):** $\Phi_{WH} = (Hours / 40)^{1.1}$ (The 1.1 exponent accounts for fatigue-related risk escalation).
*   **DT (Disruption Time):** We map worker shifts to a **Time-Risk Density Map**:
    - **Lunch/Dinner Peak:** 1.2x (High traffic congestion).
    - **Late Night (11 PM - 4 AM):** 1.3x (Low visibility, social factor risks).
    - **Standard Day:** 1.0x.

### D. Behavioral Factor ($\Omega$)
Linked to the **GigShield Trust Score**:
- **Trust Score 80-100:** 0.85x (15% discount).
- **Trust Score 40-79:** 1.0x (Standard).`
- **Trust Score < 40:** 1.2x (Penalty + manual review of all claims).

## 3. Salary Count (Affordability) Constraint

To ensure the model never burdens the worker, we implement an **Income-Linked Ceiling**:

$$P_w = \min(P_w, 0.02 \times \text{EstWeeklyEarnings})$$

| Weekly Earning | 2% Cap | Max Gold Premium | Affordability Status |
|----------------|--------|------------------|----------------------|
| ₹3,000 (Part)  | ₹60    | ₹69 (Capped at ₹60) | **Affordable** |
| ₹5,000 (Reg)   | ₹100   | ₹69              | **Affordable** |
| ₹8,000 (Super) | ₹160   | ₹69              | **Affordable** |

*Our highest premium (₹69) remains less than the cost of one chai (₹10) per day.*

## 4. Adversarial & Unique Scenarios

### The "Scenario Risk Score" ($R_{sc}$) vs. Fraud
In response to the "Market Shift" crisis (coordinated GPS spoofing), the $R_{sc}$ parameter now includes a **Systemic Anomaly Weight**:
- If a sudden spike in claims is detected in a zone without corresponding sensor data (AQI/Weather), the $R_{sc}$ for *new* policies in that zone spikes immediately. This acts as a "liquidity buffer" and discourages mass-organized fraud by increasing the entry cost for attackers.

### Disruption Time & Payout Correlation
Our model uniquely adjusts the **payout** based on the premium paid for specific times:
- If a worker pays a "Night Shift Premium", their payout per hour during a night disruption is 20% higher than a day shift payout, reflecting the higher earnings (incentives) lost during those hours.

## 5. Implementation Roadmap (AI/ML Integration)

1.  **Feature Store:** Collect platform type, shift timings, and historical trust during onboarding.
2.  **Inference:** The Python FastAPI `Premium Engine` calculates this formula in real-time when the worker views plans.
3.  **Feedback Loop:** Trust scores are updated every Monday based on the previous week's claim validation results.
